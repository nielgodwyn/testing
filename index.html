Since 1958

A working lighthouse row, turned into a place to stay.

The Trevail family kept this stretch of coast lit for three generations before opening the row of keepers' cottages to guests. We've kept the thick stone walls, the uneven floors, and the view that made the job worth having — and added the parts that make a stay comfortable: proper heating, deep baths, and a kitchen that starts early.

Every room faces water. Every stay includes breakfast, use of the honesty bar, and a printed tide table, because the beach below is only reachable for a few hours either side of low tide.
